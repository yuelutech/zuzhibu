# Roster sources

All per-person sources were accessed on 2026-10-17. Names and roles are reproduced in English without evaluation.

## Source selection

Leadership and board pages take precedence. Official company announcements and company profiles fill directory gaps. Soumith Chintala is supported by the CEO's official X appointment announcement, retrieved through X's public oEmbed endpoint. Portraits come from official publications or the image CDNs referenced by those publications.

Start dates use the documented day or month for the current principal role. A blank date means the cited sources do not establish that start month. Announcement dates without a stated effective date remain in notes rather than being converted into appointment dates.

The SEC index below lists the most recent annual report and definitive proxy of each relevant form available through the verification date. Each populated CSV SEC field points to a filing with a personal biography. Filing sections are given in the person entries. The filing date and the current role date are separate facts.
